Concept sketches for issues #2 to #6. Direction, not polish. Written suggestions are in suggestions.md beside this file.

Handoffs and the shift

The proposal: the unit of work becomes a handoff (a thread, a message, when to start, how far to go), the home screen becomes a board grouped by what you need to do, and keep-awake becomes a shift you start and end. Agents are connections with capabilities, not places you navigate to.

01 Board
Home with five agents, a shift card, and Needs you / Running / Waiting / Done sections.
02 New handoff
Which thread, when (free / at a time / now), how far (up to N / until done), and the plan sentence.
03 Shift
Keep-awake as a session: nudge, healthy, needs attention, morning report, and settings.
04 Agents
Connections list with availability and source, and an expanded agent with its capability table and threads.
05 Phone
Pairing in Settings, device list, reach options, and the trimmed board on a phone.
06 Menu bar
Icon states, two menus, the handoff submenu, and notification copy.

Every sketch has a Bone Outline toggle in the top strip. Fonts load from Google Fonts; fallbacks are Georgia and system sans.
