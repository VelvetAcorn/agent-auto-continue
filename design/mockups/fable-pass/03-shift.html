Shift settings

What a shift keeps awake

System

Always on during a shift. Prevents idle sleep while any handoff still needs the Mac.

Always

Display

Only needed for agents that cannot work with the screen locked. The app turns this on automatically for those handoffs.

Lid

macOS sleeps a closed laptop unless it is on power with an external display. The app cannot change this; it warns instead.

Warn if closed

When to give up

If the Mac sleeps anyway, missed starts catch up on wake and the board says how late they were. The shift never restarts on its own after you end it.
