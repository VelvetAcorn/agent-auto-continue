Simple direction for issue #6. One screen, plain words, settings behind a gear.

One screen, one button

The app does one thing: send Continue to a conversation at the right moment. Both sketches put that on a single screen: pick a conversation, pick when, press Continue, with the queue underneath. Every explanation becomes a tooltip, and every rarely-touched knob moves behind the gear.

01 RailA 380 px popover from the menu bar. Picker, settings and the custom time are states of the same popover. 02 App windowThe same screen as a small standalone window: compose on the left, queue on the right, settings as a sheet.

Each sketch has buttons in the top strip for the custom time, picker, settings, empty and dark states. Rendered shots are in shots/.
